Our Services

Vegetables

We export a wide variety of fresh vegetables sourced directly from trusted farmers. Our vegetables are carefully selected, cleaned, and packed to ensure freshness and long shelf life during transportation.

Rice

We supply premium quality rice varieties, including basmati and non-basmati rice. Our rice is processed hygienically and packed securely to maintain its taste, aroma, and quality.

Spices

We export authentic Indian spices such as turmeric, chili, pepper, cumin, and more. Our spices are rich in flavor and aroma, carefully processed, and packed to preserve their natural qualities.

About Company

Ayyan ExIm is a reliable and growing export company specializing in Premium quality rice, Fresh onions, Vegetables, and Spices. Based in India, we are committed to delivering farm-fresh agricultural products to international markets with the highest standards of quality and hygiene.
We work closely with trusted farmers and suppliers to source the finest produce. From carefully processed rice varieties to handpicked onions, fresh seasonal vegetables, and authentic Indian spices, every product is selected and packed with care to preserve its freshness, taste, and nutritional value.
At Ayyan ExIm, Quality is our first priority. We follow strict quality control measures, hygienic packaging practices, and efficient logistics management to ensure timely delivery and customer satisfaction worldwide.
